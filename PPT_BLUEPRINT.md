Slide-by-slide plan for the TRACE-X deck, built **exactly inside the SIH Idea-Submission template's own structure** (see [TEMPLATE_ANALYSIS.md](TEMPLATE_ANALYSIS.md)). Per the template's own instructions (its slide 7) and the decision to follow that structure precisely: **the final deck is 6 slides — no more, no fewer.** No new layout is introduced; only "Title Slide" and "Title and Content" (the two layouts the template itself already uses) are used, in the same order, with the same recurring elements (blue footer band, team badge, logo) left untouched.

Nothing here has been rendered yet. No PPTX or images are generated by this document — planning only.

## Why 6 slides, and where the 14-beat story goes

The requested narrative arc —

`PROBLEM → REACTIVE RESPONSE → SIH ASK → TRACE-X → HOW IT WORKS → FRAUD GRAPH → SPATIO-TEMPORAL PREDICTION → EXPLAINABLE RISK → INTERVENTION OPTIMIZATION → DASHBOARD → DEMO → IMPACT → FUTURE → CLOSING`

— has 14 beats. The template has 6 fixed sections. Rather than inventing slides the template doesn't have, each of the template's 5 content sections is built around **one dominant visual that itself carries 2–3 story beats at once**, so nothing from the narrative is dropped — it's compressed, not cut:

| Template slide (fixed) | Story beats it carries |
|---|---|
| 1. Title | *(identity / compliance, not a narrative beat)* |
| 2. Proposed Solution | PROBLEM → REACTIVE RESPONSE → SIH ASK → TRACE-X → HOW IT WORKS |
| 3. Technical Approach | FRAUD GRAPH → SPATIO-TEMPORAL PREDICTION → EXPLAINABLE RISK → INTERVENTION OPTIMIZATION → DASHBOARD |
| 4. Feasibility and Viability | FUTURE (scalability, roadmap, honest risk/mitigation) |
| 5. Impact and Benefits | DEMO (before/after payoff) → IMPACT |
| 6. Research and References | CLOSING |

Every slide keeps the template's locked elements exactly as documented in TEMPLATE_ANALYSIS.md §§4,7,8,12,13: the `0070C0` blue footer band, the "Your Team Name" badge (top-left), the fixed footer text, slide number, and the established font pattern (Times New Roman 36pt bold titles / Arial 24–28pt bullets). No new colors outside `0070C0` + black/white/gray are introduced.

## SLIDE 2 — Proposed Solution

1. **Slide number:** 2
2. **Purpose:** Land the core pitch in one slide — the problem, why today's response fails, what the problem statement demands, and TRACE-X's answer.
3. **Main message:** *Today: complaint → investigation, after the money is already gone. TRACE-X: complaint → prediction → intervention, while the money is still in the system.*
4. **Exact template layout:** `Title and Content` (slideLayout2.xml) — same as the source slide 2. Title placeholder replaces "IDEA TITLE"; the existing bullet textbox is replaced with the concise content below; badge and footer band stay as-is.
5. **Suggested headline:** **`STOP CHASING WITHDRAWN CASH. PREDICT WHERE IT'S HEADED.`**
6. **Concise slide content** (short lines, not paragraphs, per PPT_RULES §5):
   - **The problem:** ~8,000 cyber-fraud complaints/day. Once cash is withdrawn, the trail ends — no account to freeze, nothing to reverse.
   - **Today is reactive:** Complaint filed → investigation begins → money already gone.
   - **What SIH 26184 asks for:** a Predictive Analytics Engine + Risk Heatmap + Law Enforcement Interface + Alert System.
   - **TRACE-X:** a spatio-temporal cash-out intelligence platform — predicts **where**, **when**, and **why** stolen money will surface, and recommends **what to do about it**.
7. **Visual to create:** A single before/after flow diagram, two stacked rows:
   - Row 1 (labeled "TODAY"): `Complaint → Investigation → Cash Withdrawn → Trail Cold` (last box visually "dead"/greyed)
   - Row 2 (labeled "TRACE-X"): `Complaint → Prediction → Intervention` (last box visually "active"/highlighted in `0070C0`)
8. **What the visual must communicate:** The single structural shift that defines the whole project — investigation happening *after* the loss vs. intervention happening *before* it — understandable in three seconds, no caption needed to explain it.
9. **Data/diagram/chart required:** Simple flat two-row flowchart, 3–4 boxes per row connected by arrows. No real data — purely conceptual, flat design in `0070C0`/black/white/gray only, no gradients or generic AI imagery (per PPT_RULES §8/§9).
10. **Speaker/story purpose:** Opening of the pitch (matches PROJECT.md's "FAZIL — 0:00 to 1:00" script). Must earn the judges' attention in the first 60 seconds by naming the real gap competitors won't address.

## SLIDE 3 — Technical Approach

1. **Slide number:** 3
2. **Purpose:** Prove technical depth and differentiate TRACE-X from "a red dot on a map" — show the actual intelligence pipeline.
3. **Main message:** TRACE-X isn't one model — it's a pipeline that goes from raw complaint data to an explainable, resource-aware deployment decision.
4. **Exact template layout:** `Title and Content` (slideLayout2.xml). Title replaces "TECHNICAL APPROACH" (can stay verbatim — it already matches). Existing bullet textbox content replaced.
5. **Suggested headline:** **`GRAPH → PREDICTION → EXPLANATION → OPTIMIZATION`**
6. **Concise slide content:**
   - **Fraud Intelligence Graph** — connects accounts, transactions, devices and historical cash-outs as one network, not flat rows
   - **Spatio-Temporal Prediction** — outputs **where** (H3 hex cells) and **when** (a time window), not just a single point
   - **Explainable Risk** — every ranked location ships with its evidence (SHAP factors + graph path) — no black-box scores
   - **Intervention Optimizer** — assigns limited response teams to maximize coverage, not just "top-N riskiest"
   - **Operational Dashboard** — risk heatmap + investigator console + multi-channel alerts, human-approved before any action
   - *Core stack:* Neo4j (graph) · XGBoost/GNN + survival models (prediction) · SHAP (explanation) · H3 + PostGIS (geospatial) · React/Mapbox (dashboard)
7. **Visual to create:** A single horizontal pipeline diagram, 6 connected stages: `DATA → GRAPH → PREDICTION → EXPLANATION → OPTIMIZATION → ACTION` (directly reusing the architecture shape PPT_RULES §9 prescribes), with a one-word label under each stage matching the bullet above it.
8. **What the visual must communicate:** That the system is a deliberate, ordered pipeline with a named job at each stage — this is the visual proof of "more than a basic hotspot model," the single most important differentiation claim in the whole deck.
9. **Data/diagram/chart required:** Flat 6-box pipeline diagram with directional arrows, `0070C0` accent on stage boxes, black labels. No native chart needed here — this is a conceptual/architecture diagram, not a data plot.
10. **Speaker/story purpose:** Matches PROJECT.md's "OSHO — 1:00 to 2:00" script. This is the slide judges will scrutinize hardest for engineering credibility and for whether the "beyond a basic dashboard" claim actually holds up.

## SLIDE 4 — Feasibility and Viability

1. **Slide number:** 4
2. **Purpose:** Preempt the judges' hardest objections — data availability, scale, false positives, bias — and show a credible path from prototype to real deployment (folds in the FUTURE/roadmap beat).
3. **Main message:** The hard parts have already been thought through, not glossed over.
4. **Exact template layout:** `Title and Content` (slideLayout2.xml). Title stays "FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY" (already matches). Bullet textbox content replaced.
5. **Suggested headline:** **`WE'VE ALREADY ANSWERED THE HARD QUESTIONS`**
6. **Concise slide content** (challenge → response pairs, short labels only):
   - **Real cybercrime data is restricted** → prototype runs on synthetic transaction graphs calibrated against published NCRB/I4C statistics; a real feed drops in without touching the model layer *(explicitly labeled: Prototype / Synthetic Demonstration Data)*
   - **8,000 complaints/day** → risk computed on H3 cells not per-ATM, graph queries hop-bounded, scoring batched
   - **Wrong predictions** → a team checks a quiet location — same cost as random patrolling, zero cost of false accusation; every outcome becomes a training label
   - **Bias / over-flagging a district** → the model is explicitly audited for geographic over-flagging; outputs are investigative leads, never evidence of guilt, and always require human approval
   - **Path forward:** Foundation → Intelligence → Interface → Optimizer → Action & Polish
7. **Visual to create:** A compact 4-row "Challenge → Response" card grid (small icon or bold label per row, one line each), with a thin 5-step roadmap strip beneath it if space allows after the fit check.
8. **What the visual must communicate:** That every objection has a specific, already-considered answer — not reassurance in the abstract, but a named mitigation per named risk.
9. **Data/diagram/chart required:** Simple card/row layout, no chart. If the 5-step roadmap strip doesn't fit under the challenge/response cards without crowding, drop it from this slide rather than shrinking text below readable size (per PPT_RULES §16) — the challenge/response content is the priority.
10. **Speaker/story purpose:** Defuses the toughest Q&A before it's asked (mirrors PROJECT.md §16 Anticipated Judge Questions almost verbatim) and demonstrates maturity — this is the slide that separates "just an idea" from "a team that has stress-tested its own idea."

## SLIDE 5 — Impact and Benefits

1. **Slide number:** 5
2. **Purpose:** Make the payoff concrete with a single before/after contrast, then state who benefits and how (folds in the DEMO beat as its climax).
3. **Main message:** The difference between TRACE-X and today isn't incremental — it's the difference between a cold trail and a live one.
4. **Exact template layout:** `Title and Content` (slideLayout2.xml). Title stays "IMPACT AND BENEFITS" (already matches). Bullet textbox content replaced.
5. **Suggested headline:** **`MINUTES OF WARNING. A CHANCE TO RECOVER.`**
6. **Concise slide content:**
   - **Investigators** get a ranked, explainable deployment plan — not a red dot and a guess
   - **Banks & I4C** get an early, auditable alert through secure channels, before cash leaves the system
   - **Citizens** get a real chance at recovery instead of a closed case
   - **What we measure honestly:** top-K hit rate, time-window calibration, expected coverage gain, lead time — never raw "accuracy," and never a fabricated headline number
7. **Visual to create:** A two-column before/after comparison card:
   - Left, greyed: **`WITHOUT TRACE-X`** → `Money Withdrawn → Investigation Begins`
   - Right, highlighted in `0070C0`: **`WITH TRACE-X`** → `Complaint → Prediction → Alert → Intervention`
8. **What the visual must communicate:** The end-to-end transformation in one glance — this is the emotional and logical closing argument of the technical narrative, so it must be immediately legible without reading the surrounding bullets.
9. **Data/diagram/chart required:** Two-card side-by-side comparison, flat design, no chart. If an illustrative demo number (e.g. "87% / next 45 minutes") is used anywhere near this visual, it must be explicitly labeled as an illustrative scenario, not a claimed real result (per PPT_RULES §13 and PROJECT.md §16's own "is that number real?" answer).
10. **Speaker/story purpose:** Closes the technical/impact arc on a memorable, visual contrast right before the deck moves into credibility/closing — the moment a judge should remember walking out of the room.
